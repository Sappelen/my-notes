## Community and support

Libiry and its companion tools are written by [Marjon Wiendels](https://marjonwiendels.nl). </br>

If you  have any questions or remarks, you can:
- Talk to me on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sappelen/Libiry/issues) or [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/libiry/)

If you want to support me, you can:
- Give feedback on my work on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sappelen/Libiry/issues) or [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/libiry/)
- Talk about my stuff, in real life or online
- Give stars on Github, [here](https://github.com/sappelen/libiry) or [here](https://github.com/Sappelen/BookSpineScanner)
- [Buy me a tea](https://ko-fi.com/M4M41W3BX1) :)

If you'd like to contribute as a developer, please check out the [Developer guide](7%20Developer%20guide/index).

All help is very much appreciated!
