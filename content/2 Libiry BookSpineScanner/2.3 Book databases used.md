---
title: 2.3 Book databases used
---
You can choose one or more of the following book databases in the Settings:
  1. Open Library (API, recommended)
  2. Google Books (API, recommended)
  3. Europeana (API)
  4. Library of Congress (API)
  5. Amazon (scraping - slow)
  6. Goodreads (scraping - slow)
  7. Boekwinkeltjes.nl (scraping - slow)
  8. Bol.com (scraping - slow)

As soon as a high confidence match is found, the search is stopped.
In case of equal scores, the result that has a cover image is chosen.

Europeana is a good source for historical/European works.
Library of Congress is best for U.S.-published books, especially academic, nonfiction, and trade books.
